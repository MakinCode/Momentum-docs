# Delete Your Momentum Data

**Last updated:** [30/07/2026]

Momentum is developed by **Paul Elliott**. This page explains how to delete your data — either everything, or just your AI coach's data while keeping the rest of your account.

## Delete everything (your account)

1. Open Momentum
2. Go to **Settings**
3. Tap **Delete account**, then confirm

This permanently and immediately deletes your profile, tasks, habits, events, coach conversation history, AI memory, usage records, and your login credentials. Nothing is retained afterward — there's no retention period, and this can't be undone.

## Delete just your AI coach data (without deleting your account)

1. Open Momentum
2. Go to the **Coach** tab
3. Tap the 3-dot menu in the top right
4. Choose **Clear conversation history** and/or **Clear AI memory** (Pro tier only), then confirm

"Clear conversation history" permanently deletes everything you and your coach have said. "Clear AI memory" (Pro tier only) permanently deletes what your coach has learned about your goals, patterns, and people you've mentioned. Neither affects your tasks, habits, or profile. Both take effect immediately on our servers, with no retention period.

## Can't access the app?

If you no longer have access to the app (uninstalled, can't sign in, etc.), email **p.elliott92@live.co.uk** from the email address associated with your account and request deletion. We'll delete your data within 30 days of a verified request.
